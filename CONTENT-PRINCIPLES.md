# Curated Content Principles

## Original/reviewable content

Prefer original problem statements and learning explanations. Externally adapted content must have clear redistribution rights and provenance.

## LLD

Each curated LLD challenge should demonstrate why maintainable design matters through executable design-stress scenarios, not source-shape checks.

## DSA

Future DSA challenges can use correctness, adversarial boundaries and complexity/scale scenarios appropriate to the learning objective.

## Provenance

For externally sourced/adapted material, record source URL, license, attribution requirements, upstream revision and what was changed.

## Publishing

Content changes should be small, reviewed and automatically validated before the application consumes a new revision.
